Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and processes.

What is included in the New Year Deal?

The New Year Deal includes a two‑page website and one month of hosting. It's designed to get you online quickly with a professional presence. After the first month, hosting and maintenance plans are available separately.

How many pages are included in each website build?

Our Starter landing page provides up to six sections on a single scrolling page. The Standard website includes up to five pages, and the Expanded website includes up to ten pages with more advanced layouts and features.

What is a revision?

A revision is a round of feedback where we adjust copy, layout, or design details to fine‑tune your site. Revisions help you refine what we’ve created without changing the overall structure.

What is a redesign?

A redesign is a fundamental overhaul of your site’s structure or visual approach. It goes beyond simple edits and may require a new project scope. Redesigns are not included as part of revision rounds.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Most projects are completed within 3–5 days, depending on the complexity and how quickly we receive your content and feedback. Larger or more custom builds may take longer.

What exactly am I purchasing?

You are purchasing professional services that include the design, build, and launch of your website. The final deliverable is a live site, not ownership of any proprietary software or internal tools.

Do you offer refunds?

All sales are final once work has begun. Please review our Refund Policy for full details.

What does hosting include?

Hosting provides server space and bandwidth for your website. Maintenance plans can include software updates and security checks. Hosting does not include future edits or redesigns.

What is the difference between hosting and maintenance?

Hosting keeps your website online. Maintenance can include ongoing updates, backups, and security monitoring. Hosting alone does not cover content changes or design updates.

Are edits included after launch?

Minor text edits may be included depending on your plan, but major changes, new pages, or design changes are considered new work. A redesign involves rethinking the layout or style and requires a new project scope.

Do you provide domains?

We can acquire a domain on your behalf for an additional fee. Domains are registered through third‑party registrars and can be transferred to you. If you terminate services, we will assist with domain transfer upon request; transfer fees may apply.
