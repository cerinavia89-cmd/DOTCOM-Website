Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services and processes.

What is a revision?

A revision is a round of feedback where we adjust copy, images, or design details to fine‑tune your landing page/site. Revisions help you refine what we’ve created without changing the overall structure.

What is a redesign?

A redesign is a fundamental overhaul of your site’s structure or visual approach. It goes beyond simple edits and may require a new project scope. Redesigns are not included as part of revision rounds.

What is the typical turnaround time?

Most projects are completed within 2-3 days, depending on the complexity and how quickly we receive your content and feedback. Larger or more custom builds may take longer.

What exactly am I purchasing?

You are purchasing professional services that include the design and launch of your website and/or additional add-ons. The final deliverable is a live site hosted by Dotcom Online. You are NOT purchasing site files, coding, configurations, or ownership of any proprietary software or internal tools.

Do you offer refunds?

All sales are final on landing page/site design once work has begun. Pro-rated refunds may be issued on subscription plans. Please review our Refund Policy for full details.

What does hosting include?

Hosting provides server space and bandwidth for your website. Maintenance plans can include software updates and security checks. Hosting does not include future edits or redesigns.

What is the difference between hosting and maintenance?

Hosting keeps your website online. Maintenance can include ongoing updates, backups, and security monitoring. Hosting alone does not cover content changes or design updates.

Are edits included after launch?

Minor text and image edits may be included depending on your plan, but major changes, new pages, or design changes are considered new services. A redesign involves rethinking the layout or style and requires a new design scope.

Do you provide domains?

We can acquire a domain on your behalf for an additional fee. Domains are registered through third‑party registrars and can be transferred to you. If you terminate services, we will assist with domain transfer upon request; transfer fees may apply.
